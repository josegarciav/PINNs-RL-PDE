# Uncertainty quantification

Intervals for identified physics (`pinnrl.uq`): a Gaussian (Laplace) posterior from the surrogate's Fisher information, the region of equivalence sampled by an exploratory particle swarm, ensembles summarized with a dispersion diagnostic, loss weights from the noise model, and the calibration metrics that judge them. See the theory pages on the [Bayesian view](../theory/bayesian.md) and on [equivalence regions](../theory/equivalence_regions.md).
